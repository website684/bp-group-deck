01 · OVERVIEW

What is Incentive OS?

One platform to configure, calculate, approve and explain store incentives for every Reliance Retail format.

Video 1 · about 2 minutes · English narration with on-screen captions

The problem it solves

Each format runs incentives on its own working file. Sales, attendance and store audit results are stitched together by hand; cycles take weeks to close; associates learn their number late and rarely know why it is zero.

How it works

InputsSales bills and store totals, attendance, targets and budgets, store audits (PI, GM, POP, Mystery, Dry Shrink), employee inputs and overrides.
Rules engineOne plan per vertical or brand. Eligibility gates with reason codes, then slabs, pools or kitties.
OutputsA payout row for every employee (including ₹0 rows with reasons), the HQ dashboard, the staff mobile app and a locked payroll CSV.

Four verticals, one engine

VerticalPlan typeCycle
Digital (Reliance Digital)Per-unit slab by product family, brand and price band × department achievement multiplierMonthly
Grocery (Smart, Smart Bazaar, FreshPik)Sales and EBITDA slabs by role bucket, gated by store compliance; campaigns on topMonthly
Fashion & Lifestyle (Trends)Store pool of 1% of sales when target is beaten, split by role among eligible staffWeekly
Reliance BrandsBrand-specific: % of GSV, kitty split, service-charge pool, Sunglass Hut bill catalogueMonthly (SGH weekly gates)

Pilot proof. F&L week 1 of March 2026 reconciled to RIL’s own working file: ₹18,84,375.50 across 2,463 employees, ₹0 difference.

03 · PLAN SETUP

How to set up a new incentive plan

From a blank plan to live payouts, with every option.

The life of a plan

DRAFT → SUBMITTED → APPROVED / ACTIVE (or REJECTED back to DRAFT). Approving a plan retires any other active plan for the same vertical, mode and brand. Any edit to an active plan creates a new draft version; the live version keeps paying until the new one is approved.

Step by step

  1. Open the vertical’s Rules tab (Incentives › pick vertical › Rules) and click Create New Plan.
  2. Select vertical. Electronics, Grocery, Fashion & Lifestyle or RBL. This fixes the calculation family.
  3. Plan details. Plan name, effective from, effective to. Plans are always created as DRAFT.
  4. Rules. Depends on the vertical (see table below).
  5. Review & create. Saves the draft. Fine-tune on the plan page, then Submit for approval.

GrocerySales; EBITDA; campaign (e.g. Dairy Day)Slab grid per tier (Stores / Large Format) and role bucket; minimum achievement (Sales 95%, EBITDA 90% by default); pro-rata basis (attendance, fixed 31 days, days worked); POP amber scope (managers or whole store); OMNI/PT inclusion.

04 · DASHBOARD

What the dashboard contains, and how to read it

Where the incentive cycle stands, in one screen.

  1. Choose your view. Vertical (All, Electronics, Grocery, F&L, RBL), month, and for F&L a single week (W1–W5) or the whole month. RBL adds a brand filter.
  2. Work the checklist. Targets uploaded, store inputs entered, attendance loaded, compliance approved. Each item links to the page that resolves it.
  3. Read the headline numbers. Sales achieved vs target, incentive potential, incentive on hold, payout this month, active plans.
  4. Find the money on hold. Stores held for PI or GM, and stores below sales target, listed by name.
  5. Spot the pattern. Stores by achievement band (click a band to filter) and daily sales against target pace.
  6. Drill down. City → store → employee, with staff earning, sales and incentive; plus a Top performers tab.
05 · CALCULATION · DIGITAL

How Digital incentive is calculated

Per-unit slabs by product family, brand and price, scaled by department achievement. Monthly.

Inputs

Sales billsPOS/RPOS bills by upload or Sales API: article, brand, product family, units, price, associate.
TargetsMonthly family-level targets per store, rolled up to department.
AttendanceDaily upload, rolled up to monthly present days.
Store inputsDays the store was operational; store type (regular or new store).

Processing

  1. Exclusions. Apple, OnePlus phones and Microsoft Surface are excluded. Articles already paid by a brand campaign are not paid twice.
  2. Rate per unit. Family + brand + price band gives ₹ per unit; a brand-specific row beats a catch-all row. Example: Samsung / Oppo / Vivo phones pay ₹25 (₹500–18,000), ₹50 (₹18,001–20,000), ₹75 (above ₹20,000).
  3. Department achievement. Department actual ÷ target (Telecom + AIOT, and Large + Small Appliances, are combined), mapped to a multiplier.
  4. Gates. Store open under 15 days: no payout; 15–29 days: × days ÷ 30. A new store below 90% blocks the store. Associate needs 20 present days, and must not be on notice or disciplinary hold.
  5. Payout = Σ(rate × units) × multiplier × open-days factor.

Store and department managers

A separate manager plan: no payout below 90% of store sales target. Above it, a revenue slab and a PMI slab (SM: ₹25,000 at 90%, +₹1,000 per point to 100%, +₹2,000 per point above; DM at lower rates), plus kickers for Attach+SDA, RCP, RHP, solution selling and end-of-life stock. The total is multiplied by a CX/inventory factor (×1, ×0.75 or ×0.5) and an EBITDA factor (×0.5 if the rule is missed).

06 · CALCULATION · RELIANCE BRANDS

How Reliance Brands incentive is calculated

Each brand runs its own plan mode on the same engine. Monthly.

Brand / rolePlan modeIn one line
Superdry SM / ASMBrand slab% of store GSV by store size and achievement band, pro-rated by days worked
Superdry FCKitty splitKitty = store NSV × slab %, shared by contribution
FerragamoKitty split30% SM/ASM, 70% FC; FC on individual slab × store gate (1.0 at ≥85% store achievement, else 0.9)
Armani CaféService charge pool50% of collections shared by grade points (L1–L2: 2, L3: 3, M4–M5: 4)
Sunglass HutBill cataloguePer-bill rate × store week band (≥100%: ×1, ≥85%: ×0.5) × individual and month gates; returns subtract

Inputs

  • Store GSV / NSV actual and budget.
  • Employee inputs: payout flag (Full, Half, Ineligible, Admin sales, Inter-store movement), days worked, individual NSV target and actual, payout override.
  • Sunglass Hut bills and weekly achievement; café service charge collections; spot incentives by CSV or API.

Processing

  1. Employee flags. Ineligible, Admin sales and Inter-store movement block payout, each with a reason code.
  2. Achievement = actual ÷ budget, mapped to the brand’s slab band.
  3. Apply the plan mode: slab %, kitty split, pool share or bill catalogue.
  4. Pro-rate by days worked (or attendance, per plan setting).
  5. Signed overrides. Where a brand signs off manual adjustments, they are applied as overrides and marked on the row.

07 · CALCULATION · FASHION & LIFESTYLE

How F&L incentive is calculated

Trends: a weekly store pool (Sunday–Saturday), shared by role among eligible staff.

Inputs, every week

  • Weekly sales target per store (upload).
  • Actual sales: sum of bills for the store-week, or the store total entered by the SPOC.
  • Store inputs: gross margin target and actual, pilferage index (PI) %, SM / DM / CSA headcount.
  • Daily attendance including leave; per-week eligibility override.

Processing

  1. Sales gate. The store must beat its weekly target (actual > target), or there is no pool.
  2. PI hold. PI ≥ 0.30% holds the store-week.
  3. GM gate. GM target missed: SMs and DMs get no share; CSAs still earn.
  4. Pool = 1% of actual sales, split by role using the annexure table.
  5. Eligibility per person. At least 5 present days, no leave in the week, active, not on notice or disciplinary hold.
  6. No pro-rata. All or nothing per week; each role’s share is divided by the eligible people in that role only.

08 · CALCULATION · GROCERY

How Grocery incentive is calculated

Smart, Smart Bazaar, FreshPik: Sales and EBITDA slabs by role, gated by compliance, plus campaigns. Monthly.

Inputs

  • Store sales budget and actual; EBITDA budget and actual (upload or API).
  • Store compliance form (approved before use): Mystery audit (G/A/R), POP (G/A/R), Dry Shrink (pass/fail).
  • Employee monthly inputs: attendance, working days, absent-without-leave days, eligibility override.
  • Campaign quantities, e.g. Dairy Day articles.

Processing

  1. Role bucket. CSA, Other managerial, SM/ASM, Market manager. Large-format stores use their own tier.
  2. Minimum achievement. Sales 95%, EBITDA 90%; below that pays nothing.
  3. Slab by role and achievement band (tables below).
  4. Compliance gates (table below).
  5. Pro-rata = slab × working days ÷ divisor (plan setting).
  6. Total = Sales ₹ + EBITDA ₹ + Campaign ₹. Amounts lost to a gate are not redistributed.

10 · CONTROLS

Approvals, period lock and payroll

How a calculated number becomes a payment, with a control at every step.

  1. Maker edits a plan, version, targets or spot incentives and submits. Status becomes SUBMITTED.
  2. Checker (approval rights for that vertical) reviews in the approvals inbox: Approve → ACTIVE, or Reject with a reason → back to DRAFT.
  3. Calculate. Runs on upload, manual recalculation, plan approval or schedule; each run is logged in Run Summary.
  4. Lock the period. Choose vertical and period. A locked period refuses recalculation, input edits and uploads; its payout total is frozen. Unlocking needs a reason.
  5. Download payroll CSV. Only from a locked period, and only if the total still matches the lock-time total. Columns: employee ID, name, role, store, vertical, period start/end, amount.
  6. Audit. Every create, submit, approve, reject, upload, update, calculation, lock, unlock and export is in the audit log.
