1Purpose and background

This Statement of Work sets out the platform, functional capability, implementation approach and operating model that BetterPlace proposes to deliver to Jindal Stainless Limited for the management of its frontline and contract workforce.

Jindal Stainless operates India's largest stainless steel manufacturing business, with integrated operations at Jajpur in Odisha and Hisar in Haryana, downstream and service centre facilities, and international operations including PT Jindal Stainless Indonesia and Iberjindal in Spain. The workforce combines permanent employees with a substantial contract workforce engaged through multiple manpower contractors across melting, rolling, finishing, projects, logistics and support functions.

Two characteristics of the business make contract workforce control a material control problem rather than an administrative one. First, scale and multiplicity: contract labour is engaged through many contractors, across multiple states, under different state rules for minimum wages, factory operation and overtime. Second, the announced capacity expansion towards 4.2 MTPA, together with acquired assets being brought to fuller utilisation, means a sustained cycle of project mobilisation and demobilisation — each cycle bringing a fresh population of contractors, licences, inductions, gate passes and statutory obligations, frequently at sites where a construction phase and an operating phase overlap.

As principal employer, JSL carries obligations for that contract workforce under the Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act 1970, the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952 (including principal employer liability where a contractor defaults), the Employees' State Insurance Act 1948, the Employee's Compensation Act 1923, the Factories Act 1948 and applicable state rules. These obligations are also increasingly disclosed rather than merely held: JSL reports annually under the SEBI Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting framework, which places contract workforce, safety and worker welfare data on the public record.

The purpose of this engagement is to place the entire contract workforce lifecycle — vendor onboarding, work orders, worker verification, onboarding, gate access, attendance, overtime, payroll inputs, statutory reconciliation, billing and training — on a single configurable platform, so that JSL has one authoritative worker record per person, per site, per contractor, and a continuously current compliance position rather than a periodic reconstruction exercise.

Document intent. This SOW is written to be comprehensive. Sections 4 to 18 describe the full capability available on the platform. The modules JSL elects to deploy, and the sequence of deployment, are set out in Section 19 and may be adjusted by mutual agreement without altering the capability described here.

3Platform overview and architecture

3.1Design principle

The platform is built around a single worker record. Every module reads from and writes to the same record, so that a worker verified once is trusted everywhere, attendance computed once drives both payroll and billing, and a compliance status is a property of the record rather than a separate register maintained in parallel.

LayerFunction
Worker recordIdentity, demographic and contact data, verification status and case history, employment and deployment history across contractors and sites, skills and certifications, statutory identifiers (UAN, ESIC IP, PAN, Aadhaar reference), bank details, documents
Organisation modelLegal entity, plant/unit, department, function, role/designation, site, cost centre and reporting hierarchy at N levels; each worker holds a default role and default site
Vendor and commercial modelVendor master, licences and statutory registrations, work orders, rate cards, sanctioned strength, billing terms

3.2Access and roles

Access is role-based and permission-aware. Typical roles provisioned for a manufacturing deployment include: corporate HR/IR administrator, plant HR, plant compliance officer, department supervisor, security and gate operator, contractor/vendor user (restricted to their own workers and work orders), finance/accounts payable user, auditor (read-only), and worker. Module visibility, site scope and action rights are configured per role.

4Organisation setup and worker master

4.1Organisation configuration

  • N-level organisation hierarchy mirroring JSL's legal entities, plants, units, departments and reporting lines, including parent–child structures.
  • Site registry with country, state/region, city and site, searchable and paginated, with site-specific rules for attendance, shifts and approvals.
  • Function and role master defining designations and departments; each worker carries a default role and default site.
  • Cost centre and department mapping for payroll and billing allocation.

4.2Worker master and 360° profile

  • Employee master supporting large workforces with search across configurable fields, grid and list views, row selection, and bulk actions including bulk onboarding and export.
  • 360° worker profile consolidating personal details, role, site, contractor, verification case and status, employment tenure timeline across client / vendor / sub-vendor, documents, statutory identifiers and log history.
  • Profile lock control to freeze a record from further edits once finalised, with change history retained.
  • Data-completeness enforcement: the profile surfaces a "missing information" banner that identifies exactly which fields are blocking verification or payroll processing and links directly to the gap.
  • Full log history on the record, providing an audit trail of who changed what and when.

5Vendor management, work orders and CLRA

This section addresses the core of the contract labour control problem: who the contractor is, what they are authorised to do, how many people they may deploy, and whether they are meeting their statutory obligations.

5.1Vendor onboarding and master

  • Vendor registration with company details, GSTIN, PAN, PF and ESIC establishment codes, bank details and authorised signatories.
  • Document registry per vendor with validity tracking and expiry alerts: CLRA licence, PF and ESIC registration, GST registration, Workmen's Compensation / Employee's Compensation policy, shops and establishment registration, and any JSL-specific safety or quality certifications.
  • Per-vendor deployment rules, allowed sites, allowed roles and rate cards.
  • Vendor user access, so contractors transact on the platform directly rather than by email, while seeing only their own workers and orders.
  • Vendor performance and compliance visibility on a single dashboard across all manpower vendors.

5.2Work orders

  • Work order creation against a vendor, capturing scope of work, plant/unit and department, sanctioned headcount by skill category, rate card, validity period, billing terms and applicable compliance conditions.
  • Every deployed contract worker is mapped to a work order, so headcount, wages and billing are tracked against that order per contractor.
  • Work order lifecycle: draft, approval, active, amendment, extension, closure — with configurable multi-level approval.
  • Real-time view of deployed versus sanctioned headcount per work order and per site.
  • Amendment history retained for audit.

5.3CLRA and licensed strength control

  • Contractor licence recorded with licensed strength, validity and issuing authority.
  • Deployed worker count monitored against licensed strength; the platform flags and can block deployment or gate pass issue that would exceed the licence.
  • Support for the statutory register set maintained by the principal employer and contractor, including register of contractors, register of workmen and employment cards, generated from platform data rather than maintained separately.
  • Data and extracts to support half-yearly and annual return preparation.
  • Principal employer position visible at any time: which contractors are compliant, which are approaching licence or document expiry, and which have open gaps.
Statutory note. The platform provides the data, controls, registers and audit trail required to evidence compliance. Determination of applicability, filing of statutory returns and legal interpretation remain with JSL and its advisors. BetterPlace configures the platform to JSL's stated interpretation of applicable rules.

6Sourcing and hiring

Included where JSL elects to run contract or direct frontline hiring on the platform.

  • Requisitions and openings per role, site and city, with approval chains and vacancy tracking against filled versus total positions.
  • Configurable pipelines — screening, interview, approval, offer — matching JSL's process rather than a fixed workflow.
  • Multi-channel sourcing: branded career page, job boards, referrals, QR codes for walk-in and gate drives, vendor-submitted candidates, and WhatsApp-based application without app installation.
  • Application links and QR codes per campaign, with views, starts, applications and conversion measured; links can be scoped to an opening or to all live openings.
  • Progressive candidate profiling — role and location intent, personal details, address and pincode, education and experience, languages spoken, availability — captured in short guided steps in the candidate's chosen language, with a mobile number as the resumable identity.
  • Progressive document collection so that non-blocking documents are deferred to a later stage, protecting funnel conversion.
  • Screening and ranking with fit scores against the role rubric and explainable match reasons (distance, language, education, availability, experience).
  • Interview scheduling with structured question sets, and operational queues for scheduling, form review, evaluation, document verification, BGV and offer.
  • Offer and appointment letter generation from templates.
  • Pipeline analytics: source performance, stage conversion, ageing, SLA breach and fill rate.

7.2Operating model

  • Per-candidate check selection, so a gate-entry worker and a skilled technician can carry different packs.
  • Bulk initiation across a date range or a selected set of onboarded profiles in a single action.
  • Portfolio view with overall progress and red / amber / green segmentation, filterable by location and period, with triage queues per bucket.
  • Individual case report with per-check initiated and verified dates, colour-coded status, employment tenure timeline and downloadable PDF report.
  • Status signalling that distinguishes an initiated check from one that never started, so operations can re-trigger failures.
  • Re-verification on rehire drawn from the existing verified record.
Service levels. Identity checks typically complete within minutes. Composite case turnaround is targeted at under 24 hours for digital checks; physical address and court verification are subject to field and registry timelines and are agreed as separate SLAs in Section 23.

8Digital onboarding

  • Document collection from the worker's own phone — identity documents, certificates, bank proof — with API-based validation, configurable by state and worker category.
  • e-Sign of contracts, appointment letters and policy acknowledgements.
  • Bank and statutory setup captured once, with PF and ESI templates generated and the record made payroll-ready without re-entry.
  • Bulk onboarding by Excel upload for batch mobilisation, with per-row error reporting and correction in a single cycle.
  • Vendor-led onboarding through the same interface, so every contractor follows one standard regardless of size.
  • Platform provisioning — app access, role, site and shift assigned on completion.
  • First-day journey with role-based checklists, induction content and policies in the worker's language.
  • Onboarding status tracking across every joiner, with a funnel view identifying where a batch is held.

9Gate pass, safety induction and access control

  • Conditional gate pass issue. A pass is issued only when the governing conditions are satisfied — a valid and active work order, headcount within licensed and sanctioned strength, verification cleared to the required level, valid Workmen's Compensation cover, and a completed safety induction.
  • Safety induction delivered on the worker's phone in their language, combining standard operating procedure content, video and an assessment, issuing a dated certificate on completion.
  • Validity and expiry management for gate passes, induction certificates and insurance cover, with advance alerts and automatic withdrawal of access on expiry.
  • Biometric verification at the gate through integrated devices, with face registration performed in the platform.
  • Visitor management handled through the same flow.
  • Gate event log retained per worker per day as evidence of presence and access control.

10Attendance, rostering and overtime

10.1Capture

  • Facial recognition with AI spoof detection, preventing photograph-based and proxy marking, with a dedicated review queue for possible spoofs.
  • Geo-fenced marking so attendance registers only within the defined site boundary; each site carries its own fence.
  • Biometric and face terminals at plant gates, and mobile marking for field and project locations, both feeding the same record.
  • Face registration performed within the attendance module before facial marking is enabled.

10.2Shifts and rostering

  • Reusable shift cards defined by name and start/end time, with present and half-day values auto-calculated and a configurable tolerance buffer absorbing early or late punches.
  • Shifts created individually or uploaded in bulk; night and cross-midnight shifts supported.
  • Weekly roster grid by associate and day, with daily shift counts, site and associate filters, copy and bulk operations, undo, and an explicit publish step; Excel roster upload auto-publishes.
  • Weekly off, rest day and field designation per associate per day.
  • Associate-level view to inspect or change an individual's shifts across the week.
  • Demand planning and shortfall management across sites.

10.3Exceptions and regularisation

  • Exception filter views for log summary, irregularity, leave, no-show, review, role review and weekly-off review.
  • Regularisation workflow where the worker selects or corrects the time, reviews the associated shift, role and site, selects a predefined reason (no internet, unable to mark, manager-requested correction, wrong shift, wrong role), reviews and submits, with a mandatory reason and full audit trail.
  • Configurable self-service and manager regularisation limits.
  • Approval queue with sub-queues for regularisation, leave, optional holiday, reconciliation, attendance sync and possible spoofs, with bulk approve and reject and an SLA counter on pending items.
  • Bulk log edit for correcting many records in one operation, within an administrator-defined backdating window.
  • Manager approval of regularisation and leave from the mobile application.

10.4Overtime

  • Overtime computed automatically from attendance against the configured shift and thresholds.
  • Monthly and quarterly overtime caps configurable at organisation and site level, with alerts as thresholds approach and controls when limits are reached.
  • Pre-approval of overtime configurable as a policy switch, so hours are authorised before they are worked.
  • Overtime disallowed automatically where punch records are missing.
  • Overtime flows into payroll at the applicable statutory multiple.
  • Overtime report showing who worked overtime, how many hours and who approved it, exportable by date range and filterable by function, role and location, supporting the statutory overtime register.

10.5Policy configuration

Organisation-level configuration governs full-day and half-day hour thresholds, monthly overtime cap, login and logout tolerance, late-login and early-logout buffers, and how far back administrators may edit logs or rosters. Policy switches cover forced logout, elapsed-hour calculation from first login to last logout, associate regularisation, manager approvals, overtime pre-approval, leave management, attendance reconciliation, marking through the app, verification-status checks before marking, review of week-off attendance, weekly-off assignment on holidays, and site auto-configuration. Associate-level configuration supports open attendance, attendance cycles and restrictions based on profile parameters. Alert management covers shift-wise, overtime, leave summary, attendance sync, leave application and regularisation alerts.

11Leave and holiday management

  • Leave policies and quotas configurable by leave period, worker category and location, with balances visible to the worker.
  • Leave application from the mobile app with approval routing to the reporting manager.
  • Holiday configuration separating regional, public and optional holidays, applied to all locations or selected sites, with distinct optional-holiday rules and an approval queue.
  • Leave and holiday events reflected in attendance computation and payroll.

12Payroll and statutory compliance

12.1Payroll processing

  • Payroll computed directly from verified attendance, with no manual entry of days or hours.
  • Payroll workspace organised around payroll inputs, run payroll, final settlement, invoice, reports, compliances and reimbursements.
  • Period-controlled pay runs with a month and cycle picker, batch pipeline showing processing stage and status, and visibility of where a run is waiting or has failed before drill-down.
  • Salary structure templates by worker category and skill grade, with component-level configuration.
  • Minimum wage validation per state and skill category, applied before a structure is approved, blocking non-compliant configurations at entry.
  • Variable inputs — incentives, allowances and one-off payments — uploaded before the run and included in computation.
  • Overtime paid at the applicable statutory multiple.
  • Reimbursements, full and final settlement and exit processing within the same module.
  • Maker–checker approval, period lock and complete batch audit trail.
  • Bank-ready output files and payslips published to the worker application.

12.2Statutory compliance and vendor reconciliation

This is the control that addresses principal employer exposure directly.

  • Separate workspaces for PF, ESI, Professional Tax and Labour Welfare Fund, with scheduled filing support.
  • Vendor challan upload by contractors into the platform.
  • OCR extraction of UANs, amounts and contribution periods from uploaded challans without manual re-keying.
  • Worker-by-worker reconciliation comparing amounts actually deposited against amounts computed as due for each named worker — the level at which short payments become visible, rather than at vendor totals.
  • Automatic flagging of short payments, non-payment, incorrect UANs and late filing.
  • Principal employer notification and vendor flagging on discrepancy, creating a documented and dated record of due diligence.
  • Compliance dashboard showing, for each period, which vendors are clear, which filed late and which carry gaps.
  • Wage register and statutory register support, and an audit file that remains current rather than being assembled retrospectively.
Reference outcome. On comparable deployments this control set has reduced payroll leakage by approximately 90% and vendor non-compliance risk by approximately 80% across a base of more than 3,000 vendors. Outcomes at JSL will depend on baseline maturity and are not warranted in this document.

13Vendor billing and invoice reconciliation

  • Vendor invoices generated or uploaded against the governing work order.
  • Automatic reconciliation of invoiced manpower against verified attendance for the period and against the rate card on the work order.
  • Variance reporting identifying billed-but-not-present, rate mismatches and headcount exceeding sanctioned strength.
  • Deduction handling for advances, penalties, recoveries and statutory withholdings.
  • Compliance-gated release, where PF and ESI challan reconciliation for the period can be configured as a precondition to invoice approval.
  • Approval workflow to JSL's authorisation matrix, and posting of approved invoices to the finance system.

14Training and upskilling

  • Mobile-first learning delivered in the worker's language, with content translated into Indian languages including audio, and short-format modules suited to shift patterns.
  • Safety, statutory, technical and process training, structured into courses, levels and chapters with configurable certificates.
  • Assessments with multiple question types, configurable pass criteria, answer visibility rules and optional face-detection proctoring.
  • Mandatory course enforcement, where a worker cannot proceed in the application until required training is complete — the mechanism used to gate deployment on induction.
  • Rule-based assignment of courses and assessments by role, site or event without manual distribution.
  • Certification tracking with expiry and refresher scheduling.
  • In-app live classes for instructor-led sessions and QR-based classroom attendance for in-person training.
  • Completion, score and certification analytics by site, department, contractor and role.
  • In-house content development available where JSL supplies source material such as SOPs or existing videos.

15Reports, MIS and analytics

  • Standard reports including muster roll, attendance log report and overtime report, with date-range selection, filters for function, role, gender and location, queued generation and Excel export.
  • Statutory registers and extracts supporting CLRA registers, wage registers and returns preparation.
  • Self-serve report builder with a tags-and-filters panel, so plant teams generate what they need without raising IT requests.
  • Custom forms configurable to capture site-specific data.
  • Embedded BI dashboards for attendance analytics, payroll analytics and workforce metrics, filterable by state, city and site, including trend charts, method-and-status breakdowns and shortfall analysis by site.
  • Scheduled distribution of defined reports to named recipients.
Transparency on data currency. Operational screens reflect transactions in near real time. Embedded BI dashboards are refreshed on a processing cycle and may lag the operational database by a short interval. Where JSL requires strictly real-time reporting for a specific metric, this will be identified during design and addressed either through an operational screen or an agreed event-push integration.

25Acceptance criteria
