The problem · and why this model wins

300 million frontline workers. Almost none of the software industry builds for them.

The problem

Frontline operations run on people doing robotic work

  • ✕Onboarding takes days, screening takes weeks — humans chasing documents, reading challans, scheduling interviews by phone.
  • ✕Generic chatbots can't be trusted here. A probabilistic bot that skips step 5 of a safety induction is a liability, not a product.
  • ✕Enterprise AI is priced for desks, not for a 100,000-person deskless workforce that speaks 24 languages.
The AI Labs answer

Agents that work like employees — at machine cost

  • ✓Deterministic flows: every mandatory step runs, in order, with an audit log — safe for BFSI, pharma, manufacturing.
  • ✓Apps inside the chat: camera proof, document upload, quizzes, location — the agent doesn't just talk, it operates.
  • ✓Write-back to client systems (MCP tools) — agents act in the ATS, payroll and rosters, not beside them.
